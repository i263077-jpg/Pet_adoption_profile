How to Adopt

Follow these simple steps to welcome a pet into your home.

Adoption steps

  1. Browse the pets on our Our Pets page and choose one you would like to meet.
  2. Think about your home, schedule, budget, and the pet’s space and care needs.
  3. Contact us with the pet’s name and a little information about your household and experience with pets.
  4. Talk with us about the pet and arrange a meeting to see if you are a good match.
  5. Prepare your home and be ready to provide care for the pet throughout its life.

Before you adopt

Consider whether everyone in your household is ready for a pet, and whether you have enough time, space, and resources for food, supplies, and veterinary care. Each animal has different needs, so choose a pet whose care fits your home and daily routine.

What to tell us

When you contact us, include the pet’s name, your preferred way to be reached, who lives in your home, and any experience you have caring for animals. You can also tell us what questions you have about the pet.

A lifelong commitment

Adopting a pet is a long-term commitment to its safety, health, and wellbeing. Please make sure you are prepared to care for your new companion for its entire life.
